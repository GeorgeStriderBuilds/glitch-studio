Think of a video as a flipbook. One frame is one still picture. This makes 6 different glitch pictures, shows each one for half a second, then starts again from the first one. 6 pictures × 0.5 seconds = 3 seconds, looping, so it looks like the picture is glitching on its own.

Step 1: Generate 6 random frames. Step 2: Play preview to watch them flip. Step 3: Save animation.

Saves a WebM video, not a GIF. Open it in Chrome or a video player to see it move; some photo galleries only show the first frame.
